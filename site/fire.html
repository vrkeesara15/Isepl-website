Critical risks

Modern facilities face modern fire risks.

Most fires in commercial and industrial buildings start inside equipment: electrical panels, server racks and battery systems. Targeted, automatic suppression stops them early, before they can spread or force a shutdown.

Electrical & IT panelsOverheating, arcing and loose connections inside enclosed panels.
Lithium-ion batteriesThermal runaway in battery storage, UPS rooms and EV charging.
Data & control roomsHigh-value equipment that cannot tolerate water or downtime.
Life safetySmoke and fire spread through openings, atriums and escape routes.
Solutions

Fire suppression & protection solutions

Active suppression to stop fire at the source, and passive protection to keep escape routes and compartments safe.

Electrical & IT panel fire suppression

Automatic, self-contained suppression installed inside switchgear, MCC, PLC, server and control panels.

  • Detects and suppresses at the point of origin
  • No external power required
  • Clean agent: no residue, no damage to equipment
  • Direct and indirect discharge options

Lithium-ion battery fire suppression

Purpose-designed protection for battery energy storage, UPS battery rooms, EV charging and battery manufacturing.

  • Early detection of off-gassing and heat
  • Suppression and cooling to tackle thermal runaway
  • Rack, cabinet and container level solutions

Clean agent & inert gas systems

Total flooding systems for data centres, server rooms, control rooms and archives.

  • Safe for occupied spaces
  • No water damage to critical assets
  • Aspirating smoke detection for very early warning
  • Integration with fire alarm and BMS

Water mist, sprinkler & hydrant networks

Engineered water-based protection, from high-pressure water mist to sprinkler grids, hydrants and pump rooms.

  • Wet-pipe and pre-action sprinkler systems
  • Hydrant networks and pump rooms
  • Low water use with water mist

Fire & smoke curtains

Automatic curtains that stay hidden until needed, then deploy to compartmentalise openings, atriums and lobbies.

  • Automatic, accordion, egress and fixed types
  • Tested to EN 1634-1 and EN 12101-1
  • Up to 120 minutes integrity and radiation performance
  • Large openings with no visible barrier

Fire doors & glazed fire partitions

Rated steel, shaft and glazed fire doors for staircases, lift lobbies, shafts and corridors.

  • 30 to 120 minute ratings
  • Tested to IS 3614
  • Single and double leaf, glazed options
  • Standard and custom colours
Compliance

Designed for code. Tested to international standards.

We select and engineer systems that meet the National Building Code and the latest Part F fire and life safety provisions, together with international test standards.

  • Fire doors with 30 to 120 minute ratings for stairwells, lift lobbies, shafts and basements
  • Fire curtains tested for integrity (E), radiation (EW), insulation (EI) and smoke control (Sa)
  • Suppression designed in line with NFPA and relevant IS standards
  • Documentation that supports first-time inspection clearance
