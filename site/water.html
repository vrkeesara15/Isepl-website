Solutions

Pipelines, treatment & heating

Three capabilities, one team: distribute water and fluids reliably, treat it to the right standard, and heat it efficiently.

Thermoplastic valves & piping

Corrosion-proof ball, butterfly, diaphragm and check valves and piping in PVC-U, PVC-C, PP-H and PVDF for chemical and high-purity lines.

  • 100% corrosion resistant: no rust or chemical attack
  • Manual, pneumatic and electric actuation
  • Wide temperature range, −40°C to +140°C
  • Lower total cost of ownership than metal

Pipe repair & joining couplings

Clamp-on couplings that repair leaks, cracks and corrosion, or join pipes, without welding, threading or flanging.

  • Repair in 15 to 30 minutes
  • No hot work permit, minimal or no shutdown
  • Steel, stainless, ductile and cast iron, HDPE, PVC
  • DN50 to DN300 and larger

Special-application distribution

Engineered pipelines for process, chemical, high-purity, chilled and hot water, and fire water networks.

  • Material selection for the medium
  • Flow, pressure and thermal design
  • Instrumentation and flowmeters

Water & wastewater treatment

Engineering of treatment plants: filtration, RO/UF, softening, STP and ETP, through to zero liquid discharge.

  • Process design and equipment supply
  • Modular STP and ETP plants
  • Water reuse and recycling

Treatment chemicals

Specialty chemicals for boilers, cooling towers, RO membranes and effluent treatment.

  • Antiscalants and membrane cleaners
  • Cooling and boiler water programmes
  • Dosing systems and monitoring

Heat pumps

High-efficiency heat pumps for hot water and process heating in homes, hotels, hospitals and industry.

  • Up to 3 to 4 times more efficient than electric heating
  • Lower fuel and operating cost
  • Residential, commercial and industrial capacities
Emergency pipe repair

Repair leaks in minutes. Avoid downtime for days.

When a pipe leaks, cracks or corrodes, every minute of downtime costs money. Clamp-on repair couplings provide a fast, reliable, permanent repair, often paying for themselves in a single avoided shutdown.
