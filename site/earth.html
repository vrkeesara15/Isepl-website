Siphonic roof drainage

Smaller pipes. Level runs. No underground pipework.

In a conventional gravity system only about 30% of the pipe carries water. A primed siphonic system runs full bore, so pipes can be much smaller and run level at roof height, all the way to the storage tank or discharge point.

  • Fewer outlets and downpipes for large roofs
  • Horizontal pipework with no gradient: easy coordination with other services
  • No underground collection pipes, less excavation
  • Anti-block outlets for concrete, metal gutter and membrane roofs, 4 to 90 L/s
How it works

Four stages of siphonic flow.

As rainfall intensifies, the system primes itself, moving from gravity flow to full-bore siphonic flow with maximum capacity.

0 – 20% · Light rain

Gravity flow

Water flows along the pipe with air carried above it.

20 – 40% · Moderate rain

Plug flow

Air pockets are driven down the pipe by water plugs, keeping it self-cleaning.

40 – 70% · Heavy rain

Bubble flow

Water fills the pipe and carries air bubbles in suspension.

70 – 100% · Design storm

Full-bore flow

Air is purged and the pipe runs full, delivering far greater capacity.
