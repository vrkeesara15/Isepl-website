ONEAIR LIVING · Breathe better, live quieter

Air treated as ONE, not in pockets.

Conventional purifiers clean the air near the machine, leaving polluted zones elsewhere. Our whole-space system controls fresh air intake, filters it to medical grade and distributes it evenly, so every room gets the same quality of air.

  • Fresh, filtered air is gently pushed into the home
  • Indoor pressure stays slightly higher than outside, so unfiltered air, dust and pollutants cannot leak in
  • Indoor pollutants and CO₂ are continuously diluted
  • No hotspots, no stagnant zones and no clutter of portable devices

How it works

Prevention is better than purification.

A compact fresh-air unit outside the living space draws in outdoor air, cleans it and delivers it through discreet ducted or ductless distribution.

Draw in & filter

Outdoor air passes through multi-stage, medical-grade filtration that captures fine dust, PM2.5, pollen and allergens.

Pressurise

Filtered air keeps the home at a slight positive pressure, sealing out unfiltered infiltration through gaps, doors and windows.

Dilute & monitor

Fresh air continuously dilutes CO₂ and VOCs, while sensors track PM2.5 and CO₂ in real time.

Discreet outdoor installation

The fresh-air unit mounts on an external wall or terrace, keeping noise and maintenance out of the living space.

Fits new and existing homes

Designed for apartments and villas, both new builds and older constructions, with ducted or ductless distribution.
